# Disclosure day (2026) Movie review

This was an utter slog to go through

- the bad guys basically let them go in one scene
- they should have started with the alien abduction
- there's WWIII going on? It's too far in the background!
- pacing is horrendous, slows down way too much at too many different places
- The bad guys should have had far more man power and resources
- Colin Firth was wasted
- cat and mouse chase went on for too long
- alien tech felt like an ass-pull
- they literally just "let the disclosure happen"
- don't think religion would have any space in the conversation
- putting all the evidence online would have been far easier
- this felt old school, too old school and I mean it in a bad way
- honestly wouldn't have felt so conflicted by my govt doing this? They did it to protect us
- felt like they ran out of budget for CGI, the alien disclosure footage is pretty poor
- the whole movie was superficially intimate, nothing like Spielberg's war of the worlds, or close encounters of the third kind
- legacy media news just doesn't have this level of pull in current times tbh
- if releasing this on the internet was no such a priority why show 90% of people seeing it on their phones?
- they switched to a random anchor? Even though Emily Blunt was the one who started everything?
- honestly this should have accelerated WWIII not stopped it
- simply can't compare it with PHM or Arrival or even avatar.

+ telepathy/vision power was interesting
